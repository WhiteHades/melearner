# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Learners who keep legally obtained Course files on their own computers. They use melearner repeatedly during focused study sessions, often returning to a partially completed Lesson and expecting the app to preserve local context without an account or network connection.

## Product Purpose

melearner turns one local root folder into a responsive Library of Courses, Sections, and Lessons. It helps a learner resume, navigate, play, read, annotate, and understand Progress while leaving Course files under the learner's control. Success means the complete workflow stays fast, clear, accessible, and reliable on Linux, macOS, and Windows with no browser runtime or helper process.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and warm. The interface should feel like a well-made study instrument: trustworthy enough to disappear during focused work, editorial enough to make reading pleasant, and restrained enough to keep Course material primary.

## Anti-references

- Generic administration dashboards and interchangeable metric-card grids.
- Purple or blue SaaS glow, glass surfaces, decorative gradients, and nested cards.
- Streaming-service chrome, content marketplaces, social feeds, and gamified streak pressure.
- Novel controls that replace familiar desktop, keyboard, or accessibility behavior.
- Dense developer-tool styling that makes learning feel like system administration.

## Design Principles

1. Resume before browse. The next meaningful Lesson is always easy to reach.
2. Course material stays primary. Chrome communicates state and then gets out of the way.
3. Local state is legible. Missing files, scan warnings, Progress, and recovery actions are specific and honest.
4. Familiar desktop behavior earns trust. Standard controls, keyboard paths, focus, and assistive technology work without special knowledge.
5. Scale stays invisible. Large Libraries and documents feel like ordinary ones because data is paged, virtualized, and bounded.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The product targets WCAG AA contrast, full keyboard operation, stable screen-reader semantics, visible focus, 200 percent text scaling, 40x40 logical-pixel targets, reduced motion, and high-contrast adaptation. Installed packages must pass scripted Orca, VoiceOver, and NVDA flows.
